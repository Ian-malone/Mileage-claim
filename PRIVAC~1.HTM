Privacy Policy

Mileage Claim app  ·  Last updated: 19 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mileage Claim ("the app", "we", "us") collects, uses, and stores your information. We take your privacy seriously and only collect what is necessary to provide the service.

1. Who we are

Mileage Claim is operated by Ian Malone. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at: ianmalone@hotmail.co.uk

2. What data we collect

We collect the following information when you use the app:

  • Account information — your email address, used to create and access your account.
  • Profile information — your full name, vehicle registration, mileage rate, claim period preference, and mileage rounding preference. This is optional but recommended for generating claims.
  • Journey records — date, start location, end location, journey purpose, and distance in miles.
  • Expense records — date, category, description, and amount.
  • Saved journeys — named routes you save for quick re-use, including start and end locations, purpose, and distance.

We do not collect your precise GPS location. Location information (postcodes or place names) is only stored if you type it in yourself.

3. How we use your data

Your data is used solely to provide the app's features:

  • Logging and displaying your journeys and expenses
  • Generating mileage claim reports as PDF documents
  • Calculating claim values based on your mileage rate

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than operating the app.

4. Third-party services

The app uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — our database and authentication provider. Your account data, journeys, expenses, and profile are stored on Supabase servers. Supabase is GDPR-compliant. See their privacy policy at supabase.com/privacy.
  • Google Maps Distance Matrix API — used optionally when you tap "Look up driving distance". The start and end locations you enter are sent to Google to calculate the driving distance. We do not store or log these requests beyond the distance result shown to you. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Data storage and security

Your data is stored securely on Supabase servers. Each user's data is isolated and can only be accessed by that user's account. We use industry-standard authentication and do not store your password in plain text.

6. Data retention

Your data is retained for as long as you have an account with us. If you wish to have your data deleted, please contact us at ianmalone@hotmail.co.uk and we will remove it within 30 days.

7. Your rights (UK GDPR)

If you are based in the UK or European Economic Area, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict how we process your data
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at ianmalone@hotmail.co.uk.

8. Children

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
